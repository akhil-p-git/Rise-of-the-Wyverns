# Audio Shortlist (CC0 & CC-BY Field Recordings and Packs)

Currently, all 23 cues in `data/audio.json` are backed by project-owned mathematical synthesis generated by `tools/gen_sfx.py` (no AI-generated audio; normalized to `-16 LUFS` for music, `-22 LUFS` for ambience, and `-3 dBFS` peak for SFX).

Because direct external file downloads are blocked in the sandboxed environment, below is the verified shortlist of CC0 / CC-BY field-recording and foley packs that Akhil can download and drop into `assets/audio/{music,ambience,sfx}` (or keep until the final soundtrack is commissioned):
