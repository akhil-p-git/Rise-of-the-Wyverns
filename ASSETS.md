## Rules
- Prefer CC0 or paid packs with commercial licenses. Keep the license file next to each pack.
- Final art for the hero, town and key enemies comes from a paid pack or a commissioned artist, not from AI generation.
- Mark anything AI-generated or AI-assisted in the Notes column.
